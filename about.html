Our Story

Discover the passion behind Al-Attar Market

Welcome to Al-Attar Market

Founded in 2016, Al-Attar Market has grown from a small neighborhood store into the premier destination for Egyptian groceries in the Menia government. Our mission is to bring the authentic flavors of Egypt straight to your table.

We carefully source our products from trusted suppliers, ensuring the highest quality and authenticity. From fresh produce to specialty items you can't find anywhere else, we've got everything you need to create delicious Egyptian meals at home.

Our knowledgeable staff is always ready to help you find what you need or make recommendations. We take pride in our customer service and the relationships we've built with our community over the years.

Our Mission

To provide our community with the highest quality Egyptian products while maintaining authentic flavors, competitive prices, and exceptional customer service.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted source for Egyptian groceries in Egypt, known for our authenticity, quality, and community engagement.

Our Core Values

Quality

We carefully select all our products to ensure they meet our high standards of quality and freshness.

Authenticity

We bring you genuine Egyptian flavors just like you'd find back home, with traditional recipes and ingredients.

Community

We're more than a store - we're a gathering place for our community to connect and share their culture.

Service

Our friendly and knowledgeable staff is always ready to help you find what you need or make recommendations.
